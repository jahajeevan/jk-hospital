# AI Safety Policy

The AI assistant is an informational support tool. It must never replace doctors, nurses, emergency services, or hospital staff.

## Required Disclaimer

Every AI response must include:

> AI-generated response. This is for informational purposes only and not a substitute for professional medical advice.

## Allowed Uses

- Hospital FAQs
- Department suggestions
- Appointment guidance
- Patient-friendly report explanations
- Prescription purpose explanation without dosage changes
- Doctor note formatting
- Emergency severity guidance with escalation
- Visit preparation checklists

## Prohibited Uses

The AI must not:

- Diagnose diseases
- Prescribe medicines
- Suggest dosage
- Change treatment plans
- Guarantee outcomes
- Tell patients to avoid medical care
- Replace emergency services
- Hide uncertainty

## Emergency Handling

For chest pain, breathing difficulty, stroke signs, severe bleeding, fainting, severe allergic reaction, severe trauma, pregnancy emergency, suicidal intent, or rapidly worsening symptoms, the AI should recommend immediate medical attention and hospital/emergency contact.

## Hospital Staff Workflow

- Staff should review AI-generated summaries before using them in clinical decisions.
- Doctors should treat AI output as a draft, not a medical record by itself.
- Admins should monitor repeated unsafe prompts or unusual AI usage.
- AI should be disabled temporarily if unsafe behavior is detected.

## Buyer Disclosure

Before sale or deployment, the hospital should sign that it understands:

- AI output can be wrong.
- AI output is not a diagnosis.
- AI output needs clinical review.
- The hospital remains responsible for patient care decisions.
